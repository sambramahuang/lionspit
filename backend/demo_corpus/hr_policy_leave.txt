FIRM HR POLICY - ANNUAL LEAVE

Date last updated: 1 January 2024
Applies to: All fee-earners and support staff

Employees are entitled to eighteen (18) days of annual leave per calendar
year, pro-rated for employees who join partway through the year. Leave
requests should be submitted via the HR portal at least five (5) working
days in advance, except in cases of emergency.

Unused leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of five (5) days into
the following calendar year, and must be used by 31 March or it will be
forfeited. This is an internal HR policy document, not a client-facing
precedent.
