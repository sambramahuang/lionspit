ALPHA ROBOTICS PTE LTD
SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT

Version: v2
Date: 5 January 2023
Status: Reviewed by associate. Not yet partner-approved. Superseded by v3.

This Shareholders' Agreement is made among the shareholders of Alpha
Robotics Pte Ltd, a company incorporated in Singapore.

1. Board Composition. The Board shall comprise four (4) directors, of
whom two shall be nominated by the majority shareholder and one each by
the two minority shareholders.

2. Transfer Restrictions. No shareholder may transfer shares without
first offering them to the other shareholders (right of first refusal),
for a period of 21 days, followed by a tag-along right for minority
shareholders.

3. Indemnity. Each shareholder indemnifies the company against losses
arising from that shareholder's breach of this agreement, capped at
S$100,000.

4. Reserved Matters. Unanimous consent required for: issuance of new
shares, amendment of the constitution, voluntary winding up, and any
related-party transaction above S$20,000.

5. Deadlock. In the event of a deadlock at Board level, either party may
refer the matter to mediation before any other remedy is pursued.
