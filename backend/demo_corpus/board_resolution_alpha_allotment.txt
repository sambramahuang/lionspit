ALPHA ROBOTICS PTE LTD
DIRECTORS' RESOLUTION IN WRITING

Date: 20 June 2024
Status: Executed. Partner-approved by S. Ho.

WE, the undersigned, being all the directors of Alpha Robotics Pte Ltd
entitled to receive notice of a meeting of directors, RESOLVE:

1. THAT the Company allot and issue 1,250,000 new ordinary shares to
Orbit Ventures Fund II L.P. at a subscription price of S$1.60 per share,
pursuant to the Series A Subscription Agreement.

2. THAT the register of members be updated to reflect the allotment
and that share certificates be issued accordingly.

3. THAT any one director be authorised to sign all documents necessary
to give effect to these resolutions, including filings with the
Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA).
