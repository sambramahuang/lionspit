GAMMA RETAIL PTE LTD (LANDLORD) AND MERIDIAN COFFEE PTE LTD (TENANT)
TENANCY AGREEMENT - RETAIL UNIT (RENEWAL)

Date: 1 November 2023
Jurisdiction: Singapore
Status: Executed. Partner-approved by S. Ho. Current.

1. Premises. Unit #01-22, Gamma Mall, Singapore, approximately 850 sq ft.

2. Term. Three (3) years commencing 1 March 2023, with an option to
renew for a further two (2) years on the same terms save as to rent.

3. Rent. Monthly rent of S$11,800, exclusive of GST, payable in advance,
with a rent review at the start of any renewal term based on prevailing
market rate.

4. Permitted Use. Retail sale of specialty coffee, light refreshments,
and pre-packaged retail goods.

5. Service Charge. Tenant shall pay a monthly service charge of S$1,200
towards common area maintenance.

6. Reinstatement. The Tenant shall reinstate the premises to its
original condition at the expiry or earlier termination of the term,
fair wear and tear excepted.

7. Break Clause. Either party may terminate this Agreement on six (6)
months' written notice after the 18th month of the term.
