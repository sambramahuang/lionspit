BETA VENTURES HOLDINGS PTE LTD
SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT

Version: v1 (final)
Date: 14 July 2021
Status: Executed. Partner-approved by M. Tan (retired 2023).

This Shareholders' Agreement is made among the shareholders of Beta
Ventures Holdings Pte Ltd, a Singapore-incorporated holding company, in
respect of a family-owned trading business.

1. Board Composition. The Board shall comprise three (3) directors, all
nominated by the majority shareholder, with one seat reserved for an
independent director.

2. Transfer Restrictions. Shares may only be transferred to existing
shareholders or their immediate family members, subject to a right of
first refusal exercisable within 45 days.

3. Dividend Policy. Not less than 40% of distributable profits shall be
declared as dividends each financial year, subject to solvency
requirements.

4. Reserved Matters. Unanimous shareholder consent required for: sale
of substantially all assets, amendment of the constitution, and
admission of any new shareholder.
