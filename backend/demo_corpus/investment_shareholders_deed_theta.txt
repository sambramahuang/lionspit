THETA BIOSCIENCES PTE LTD
INVESTMENT AND SHAREHOLDERS DEED

Date: 2 September 2023
Status: Executed. Partner-approved by S. Ho.

This Deed is entered into among the investors and founders of Theta
Biosciences Pte Ltd, a Singapore-incorporated company, in connection
with a seed investment.

Note: this firm's precedent library files this instrument type under
"Investment and Shareholders Deed" rather than "Shareholders' Agreement",
though it performs the same function and should be surfaced by a
shareholders' agreement search.

1. Governance. The founders retain board control until a qualifying
financing event, after which the lead investor is entitled to appoint
one director.

2. Pre-emption. Existing holders have a right of pre-emption over any
new issue of shares, pro rata to their existing holding, exercisable
within 14 days of notice.

3. Founder Vesting. Founder shares vest monthly over four years with a
twelve-month cliff, subject to acceleration on a change of control.

4. Liquidation Preference. The investor is entitled to a 1x
non-participating liquidation preference ahead of ordinary shareholders
on any exit.

5. Information Rights. The company shall provide unaudited quarterly
management accounts and an annual budget to the lead investor.
