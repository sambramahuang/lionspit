Status: Draft. Not executed. Not partner-reviewed.

This draft Shareholders' Agreement is made among the shareholders of
Alpha Robotics Pte Ltd, a company incorporated in Singapore.

1. Board Composition. The Board shall comprise three (3) directors, of
whom one shall be nominated by each founding shareholder.

2. Transfer Restrictions. No shareholder may transfer any shares without
first offering them to the other shareholders on the same terms
(right of first refusal), for a period of 30 days.

3. Indemnity. Each shareholder indemnifies the company against losses
arising from that shareholder's breach of this agreement, capped at
S$50,000.

4. Reserved Matters. The following require unanimous shareholder consent:
issuance of new shares, amendment of the constitution, and voluntary
winding up.
