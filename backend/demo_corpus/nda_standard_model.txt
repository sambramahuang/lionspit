MODEL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (MUTUAL)
FIRM PRECEDENT LIBRARY - MODEL CLAUSE SET

Date last reviewed: 1 May 2023
Status: Model / template. Not tied to a specific client. Partner-approved
as the firm's standard starting point by S. Ho.

This Agreement is intended as the firm's standard mutual NDA starting
point for commercial discussions of general application.

1. Definition of Confidential Information. Information disclosed by
either party, whether orally or in writing, that is designated
confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

2. Exclusions. Information that is public, independently developed, or
lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

3. Permitted Use. Confidential Information may only be used to evaluate
the proposed transaction and may not be disclosed to third parties
except to advisors under equivalent confidentiality obligations.

4. Term. Confidentiality obligations survive for two (2) years from the
date of disclosure, save for trade secrets, which survive indefinitely.

5. Governing Law. [To be inserted based on the transaction - Singapore
law is the firm's default unless a client specifies otherwise.]
