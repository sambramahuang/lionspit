ZETA TRADING PTE LTD v. HARBOUR LOGISTICS PTE LTD
PRIVILEGED AND CONFIDENTIAL - SETTLEMENT MEMO

Date: 1 March 2024
Practice Area: Dispute Resolution / Litigation
Jurisdiction: Singapore
Status: Final. Partner-approved by S. Ho. RESTRICTED - settlement terms
subject to a confidentiality clause; access limited to the deal team.

This memo records the settlement reached between Zeta Trading Pte Ltd and
Harbour Logistics Pte Ltd, superseding the demand letter dated 10
February 2024.

1. Settlement Sum. Harbour Logistics Pte Ltd agrees to pay Zeta Trading
Pte Ltd S$140,000 in full and final settlement, payable in three monthly
instalments.

2. Mutual Release. Each party releases the other from all claims arising
out of the Distribution Agreement dated 5 June 2022.

3. Confidentiality. The terms of this settlement, including the
settlement sum, are strictly confidential and shall not be disclosed
except as required by law.

4. No Admission. This settlement is made without any admission of
liability by either party.
