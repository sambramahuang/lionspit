ZETA TRADING PTE LTD v. HARBOUR LOGISTICS PTE LTD
LETTER OF DEMAND

Date: 10 February 2024
Practice Area: Dispute Resolution / Litigation
Jurisdiction: Singapore
Status: Sent. Not partner-approved as a precedent (matter-specific facts).

We act for Zeta Trading Pte Ltd ("our client") in connection with the
Distribution Agreement dated 5 June 2022 between our client and Harbour
Logistics Pte Ltd ("your client").

Our client's position is that your client breached clause 8.2 of the
Distribution Agreement by failing to maintain the agreed minimum
inventory levels, causing our client to suffer losses estimated at
S$186,000 in lost sales during Q4 2023.

We are instructed to demand payment of S$186,000 within fourteen (14)
days of the date of this letter, failing which our client reserves the
right to commence proceedings without further notice, including a claim
for interest and costs.

This letter is written without prejudice to our client's rights, all of
which are expressly reserved.
