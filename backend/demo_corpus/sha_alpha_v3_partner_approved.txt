ALPHA ROBOTICS PTE LTD
SHAREHOLDERS' AGREEMENT

Version: v3 (current)
Date: 18 June 2024
Status: Executed. Partner-approved by S. Ho.

This Shareholders' Agreement is made among the shareholders of Alpha
Robotics Pte Ltd, a company incorporated in Singapore, following the
Series A investment round.

1. Board Composition. The Board shall comprise five (5) directors: two
nominated by the founders, two nominated by the Series A investor, and
one independent director agreed by both sides.

2. Transfer Restrictions. No shareholder may transfer shares without
first offering them to the other shareholders (right of first refusal),
for a period of 21 days, followed by tag-along rights for all minority
shareholders and drag-along rights for a sale approved by holders of 75%
of shares.

3. Indemnity. Each shareholder indemnifies the company against losses
arising from that shareholder's breach of this agreement. The aggregate
liability of any shareholder under this clause is capped at S$150,000,
save in cases of fraud or wilful misconduct.

4. Reserved Matters. Unanimous consent of the Board, including at least
one investor-nominated director, is required for: issuance of new
shares, amendment of the constitution, voluntary winding up, incurring
debt above S$200,000, and any related-party transaction above S$20,000.

5. Deadlock. In the event of a deadlock at Board level, either party may
refer the matter to mediation before pursuing any other remedy. If
mediation fails within 30 days, either party may trigger the buy-sell
("Russian roulette") mechanism in Schedule 3.

6. Anti-Dilution. In the event of a down round, the Series A investor's
shares carry broad-based weighted average anti-dilution protection as
set out in Schedule 2.
