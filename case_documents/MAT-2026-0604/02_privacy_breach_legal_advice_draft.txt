1. INITIAL ASSESSMENT
The evidence presently indicates unauthorised access to a limited diagnostic export. The legal assessment depends on the number of affected individuals, whether the data was copied or merely viewed, and the sensitivity of the diagnostic fields.

2. CONTRACTUAL DUTIES
CloudMosaic must notify Northstar without undue delay, preserve logs and assist with a risk assessment. Northstar must document its decision on whether notification to the regulator or affected individuals is required.

3. RECOMMENDATION
Complete the access-log review, obtain a signed incident statement from CloudMosaic, identify affected records, and prepare a holding statement. Do not make a final notification decision until the scope is verified.
