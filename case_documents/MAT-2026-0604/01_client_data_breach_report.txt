NORTHSTAR & VALE LLP
CLIENT INCIDENT REPORT

Matter: MAT-2026-0604
Date: 2026-02-06
Client: Northstar Health Systems Pte Ltd
Counterparty: CloudMosaic Solutions Pte Ltd
Practice area: Technology, privacy and data protection
Jurisdiction: Singapore
Document type: Other
Confidentiality: Restricted

Northstar Health Systems Pte Ltd reports that an employee of CloudMosaic Solutions Pte Ltd, its hosted patient-portal provider, may have accessed a diagnostic export outside the agreed support ticket. The export contained patient identifiers and appointment information but no payment card data.

The initial investigation indicates access between 2 and 4 February 2026. CloudMosaic has disabled the account, preserved access logs and appointed an incident manager. Northstar requires advice on contractual notification duties, PDPA assessment, patient communications and preservation of evidence.

The incident is being handled under the technology services agreement and the data-processing schedule. This report is privileged and should not be circulated outside the incident response team.
