1. FINDING
The completed log review shows that 184 patient records were viewed by a support account without a business need. The records contained names, patient identifiers and appointment information. No clinical diagnosis, payment data or download event has been identified.

2. CONTRACTUAL RESPONSE
CloudMosaic breached the access-control and incident-notification provisions of the technology services agreement. Northstar should issue a reservation of rights, require an independent access-control review and preserve its indemnity claim.

3. PRIVACY RESPONSE
Northstar should document the risk assessment, notify the regulator within the applicable period if the prescribed threshold is met, and contact affected individuals using the approved notice. The notice should describe the event without speculating about motive.
