BOARD SETTLEMENT AUTHORITY NOTE

Matter: MAT-2026-0602
Date: 2026-04-10
Client: Meridian Infrastructure Holdings Pte Ltd
Counterparty: Eastbank Utilities Ltd
Practice area: Dispute resolution and energy regulation
Document type: Other
Confidentiality: Restricted

The board has authorised settlement discussions within the following parameters: Meridian may offer service credits up to SGD 420,000, a three-month independent performance review and a revised maintenance reporting protocol. Meridian must not accept termination of the Framework Agreement or release claims for unpaid invoices without further board approval.

The proposal is commercially preferable to urgent proceedings because Eastbank remains a key offtaker and the alleged shortfall is capable of technical verification. Any settlement must preserve Meridian's rights against subcontractors and must not affect the separate MAT-2026-0601 asset sale obligations.

This note records internal authority only and is not to be circulated to Eastbank.
