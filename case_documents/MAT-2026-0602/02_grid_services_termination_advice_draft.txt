1. QUESTION
Whether Eastbank may terminate the Grid Services Framework Agreement immediately after Meridian missed the January availability target.

2. ANALYSIS
Clause 14 permits termination for a material breach only if the breach is not remedied within 30 days after notice. The service-credit mechanism in Clause 9 is expressed as the exclusive financial remedy for an isolated availability shortfall. On the current evidence, Eastbank has not shown that the shortfall is persistent or material.

3. RECOMMENDED RESPONSE
Meridian should reject immediate termination, provide the requested operational logs, and propose an independent verification of the availability calculation. It should reserve all rights and avoid characterising the shortfall as an admission of breach.
