Dear Sirs,

We act for Meridian Infrastructure Holdings Pte Ltd. Your letter dated 27 February 2026 purports to terminate the Grid Services Framework Agreement with immediate effect. Our client does not accept that the termination is valid.

The alleged availability shortfall is disputed. The Agreement requires a notice identifying the breach and a 30-day period to remedy it. Your letter does neither. It also ignores the agreed service-credit regime and the maintenance exclusion in Schedule 2.

Please confirm by 12 March 2026 that Eastbank will withdraw the termination notice and participate in the independent calculation procedure. Our client reserves all rights, including claims for unpaid service fees and losses caused by wrongful termination.
