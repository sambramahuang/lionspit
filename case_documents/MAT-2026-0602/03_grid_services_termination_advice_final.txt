1. CONCLUSION
Eastbank is unlikely to have a present right to terminate the Grid Services Framework Agreement without first giving a contractual breach notice and the 30-day cure period required by Clause 14.

2. REASONS
The January availability figure is disputed and may be affected by scheduled maintenance excluded from the calculation. Clause 9 provides service credits for an isolated shortfall. The termination right is not expressed to arise from any single calculation dispute and must be construed with the cure mechanism.

3. NEXT STEPS
Respond within five Business Days, attach the verified meter data, invite an independent calculation and reserve Meridian's rights. Do not concede that the event is a material breach. If Eastbank issues a termination notice, seek an urgent declaration and injunctive relief.
