1. GOVERNANCE
The board shall comprise five directors, of whom Cedar may nominate two, Foundry may nominate one, and the founders may nominate two while they hold at least 12% of the issued shares.

2. RESERVED MATTERS
The Company shall not, without Investor Consent, issue shares, incur borrowings above SGD 2,000,000, dispose of material intellectual property, approve an annual budget materially outside the agreed plan, or enter a related-party transaction.

3. TRANSFERS AND ROFO
A shareholder proposing to transfer shares must first offer them to the other shareholders pro rata. Transfers to an affiliate are permitted if the affiliate signs a deed of adherence.

4. DRAG AND TAG
If holders of at least 70% accept a bona fide third-party offer, they may require all shareholders to sell on the same terms. Minority shareholders may participate in a sale of more than 50% of the Company.

5. FOUNDER LEAVER
Founder shares vest over 48 months. A good-leaver receives fair market value; a bad-leaver receives the lower of subscription price and fair market value.
