1. GOVERNANCE
The board consists of five directors. Cedar may nominate two directors, Foundry one director, and the founders two directors while the founders collectively hold at least 12% of the issued shares.

2. RESERVED MATTERS
Investor Consent is required for a new share issue, borrowing above SGD 2,000,000, disposal of material intellectual property, a material deviation from the approved budget, or a related-party transaction.

3. TRANSFERS
A proposed transfer is subject to a right of first offer in favour of the other shareholders. Affiliate transfers are permitted only if the transferee executes a deed of adherence.

4. EXIT
A holder or group holding 70% may exercise the drag right following acceptance of a bona fide arm's-length offer. Each minority shareholder has a tag right on a sale of more than 50%.

5. FOUNDER VESTING AND DEADLOCK
Founder shares vest over 48 months. A deadlock must proceed through senior negotiation, mediation in Singapore and, if unresolved, the buy-sell mechanism in Schedule 4.

SIGNED by the Company and each shareholder. This executed agreement supersedes the partner redline and governs the parties' relationship.
