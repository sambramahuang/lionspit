BOARD RESOLUTIONS - SERIES B COMPLETION

Matter: MAT-2026-0603

IT WAS RESOLVED that the Company accept the subscription by Cedar Ventures Fund II LP for 2,400,000 Series B preference shares at SGD 5.00 per share; allot and issue those shares subject to receipt of the subscription price; adopt the amended constitution; and approve the executed Shareholders Agreement dated 30 June 2026.

IT WAS FURTHER RESOLVED that Priya Nair and Foundry Labs Pte Ltd be authorised to sign the deed of adherence and that the officers update the register of members, lodge the prescribed filings and maintain the investor information rights register.

The board noted that the transaction proceeds are to fund product development, Australian regulatory expansion and working capital. The resolutions were passed unanimously and remain effective.
