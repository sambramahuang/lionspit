1. INSTRUCTION
HelioGrid Energy Pte Ltd (HelioGrid) has asked the firm to advise on its proposed acquisition of the distributed-solar business of Meridian Infrastructure Holdings Pte Ltd (Meridian). The proposed transaction is an acquisition of selected assets, customer contracts, intellectual property and employees, rather than a purchase of Meridian's shares.

2. COMMERCIAL OBJECTIVES
The client wishes to preserve the target's power-purchase contracts, obtain an orderly transition of permits and avoid inheriting Meridian's legacy liabilities. The indicative consideration is SGD 18,500,000, subject to completion accounts and a holdback for unresolved grid-connection claims.

3. PRINCIPAL RISKS
The transaction team must review change-of-control clauses, environmental permits, title to battery systems, employee transfer requirements and any outstanding claims by the target's largest customer, Eastern Grid Services Pte Ltd.

4. DOCUMENT PLAN
The intended sequence is: (a) asset purchase agreement draft v1; (b) partner-reviewed redline v2; (c) executed asset purchase agreement v3; and (d) completion memorandum confirming transfer of the acquired contracts and assets.

This note is an internal instruction record and is not legal advice to any person other than HelioGrid.
